# Pulsar, Home Assistant and Music Assistant

Research for [issue #3](https://github.com/RisinT96/pulsar/issues/3), 2026-10-31. Pulsar findings refer to commit [`fd434437d2aa94a1c522df7ea8ae8ed0818d7de8`](https://github.com/RisinT96/pulsar/tree/fd434437d2aa94a1c522df7ea8ae8ed0818d7de8). This is a source review, not a test of the owner's station or Home Assistant.

## Recommendation

Start with Pulsar's existing ESPHome voice satellite and Sendspin player. Configure an English Assist pipeline with local Speech-to-Phrase, the built-in conversation agent, and local Piper TTS on Home Assistant; select this pipeline for the satellite. Use Music Assistant's own Sendspin player for music, with the HA Music Assistant integration for HA-side control. No replacement wake-word engine or separate microphone-to-Wyoming bridge is needed for this design. Check the precise app availability and version against the owner's installation: it has not been inspected. [HA local setup](https://www.home-assistant.io/voice_control/voice_remote_local_assistant/), [HA voice architecture](https://contributors.home-assistant.io/architecture/platform/voice/), [Pulsar README](https://github.com/RisinT96/pulsar/blob/fd434437d2aa94a1c522df7ea8ae8ed0818d7de8/README.md#music-assistant).

## Voice path and boundaries

1. On the Mini 2, the microphone front end uses the station's proprietary local VQE and wake models. The advertised choices in this Pulsar revision are **Алиса** and **Яндекс**, tagged Russian; English is the *command* language, not a claim that an English wake word is available. The stock local command spotter also handles Russian commands, independently of HA. See [model configuration](https://github.com/RisinT96/pulsar/blob/fd434437d2aa94a1c522df7ea8ae8ed0818d7de8/satellite/crates/pulsar/src/controller/mod.rs), [front end](https://github.com/RisinT96/pulsar/blob/fd434437d2aa94a1c522df7ea8ae8ed0818d7de8/satellite/crates/pulsar-audio/src/frontend.rs), and [README local commands](https://github.com/RisinT96/pulsar/blob/fd434437d2aa94a1c522df7ea8ae8ed0818d7de8/README.md).
2. After local detection, Pulsar starts an HA voice request and sends its 16 kHz processed microphone audio, including optional pre-roll, over its ESPHome native API connection. HA executes the selected Assist pipeline; its speech integration talks to Speech-to-Phrase. HA's documented local setup discovers Speech-to-Phrase and Piper using Wyoming. [Pulsar voice controller](https://github.com/RisinT96/pulsar/blob/fd434437d2aa94a1c522df7ea8ae8ed0818d7de8/satellite/crates/pulsar/src/controller/voice.rs), [API server](https://github.com/RisinT96/pulsar/blob/fd434437d2aa94a1c522df7ea8ae8ed0818d7de8/satellite/crates/pulsar-esphome/src/server.rs), [HA local setup](https://www.home-assistant.io/voice_control/voice_remote_local_assistant/).
3. HA provides the speech response as a media URL. Pulsar fetches and plays that URL on its announce channel and signals completion. The server does **not** advertise the speaker feature and ignores incoming `VA_AUDIO`: do not assume HA sends the answer as raw audio through the same ESPHome voice stream. [Voice controller](https://github.com/RisinT96/pulsar/blob/fd434437d2aa94a1c522df7ea8ae8ed0818d7de8/satellite/crates/pulsar/src/controller/voice.rs), [API commands](https://github.com/RisinT96/pulsar/blob/fd434437d2aa94a1c522df7ea8ae8ed0818d7de8/satellite/crates/pulsar-esphome/src/server/commands.rs).
4. Playback music and announcements have separate handling; Pulsar ducks music during dialog and announcements, and coordinates local HA playback with Sendspin. [Pulsar media controller](https://github.com/RisinT96/pulsar/blob/fd434437d2aa94a1c522df7ea8ae8ed0818d7de8/satellite/crates/pulsar/src/controller/media.rs), [ducking logic](https://github.com/RisinT96/pulsar/blob/fd434437d2aa94a1c522df7ea8ae8ed0818d7de8/satellite/crates/pulsar/src/controller/logic.rs).

English Speech-to-Phrase is suitable for the selected initial goal of home-control phrases. It is constrained to known sentences and entity names, rather than a general speech recognizer. HA documents that wildcards and open-ended phrases are not generally supported out of the box. The project distinguishes the older v1.4.3 Kaldi app from the newer OHF-Voice/apps CTC rewrite, which also lists English; don't assume that configuration examples for one version apply to the other. [HA Speech-to-Phrase explanation](https://www.home-assistant.io/blog/2025/02/13/voice-chapter-9-speech-to-phrase/), [project status](https://github.com/OHF-Voice/speech-to-phrase/issues/149), [language list and legacy configuration](https://github.com/OHF-Voice/speech-to-phrase).

## Music

Music Assistant discovers Pulsar as a Sendspin player; it streams audio to that player and offers local player control, while Pulsar exposes a separate HA media entity. Sendspin supports synchronization, but Pulsar's README says group synchronization has not been validated. Ensure HA uses the intended MA player/entity and avoid accidentally treating both advertised entities as separate physical speakers. [Pulsar README](https://github.com/RisinT96/pulsar/blob/fd434437d2aa94a1c522df7ea8ae8ed0818d7de8/README.md#music-assistant), [MA Sendspin](https://www.music-assistant.io/player-support/sendspin/).

MA documents native HA Search and Play support from HA 2025.6 onward. However, arbitrary spoken titles and artists require the speech recognizer to return those words: a text-based Assist test or a supported intent does not demonstrate that constrained Speech-to-Phrase recognizes arbitrary titles. For initial acceptance, test playback initiated in the MA UI or HA and basic supported voice transport commands; test any named music requests explicitly. Finite named playlists/custom sentences might be possible depending on the installed Speech-to-Phrase version and grammar, but require a separate proof. An open-ended recognizer such as Whisper is an option for a later requirement, subject to HA host capacity. [MA voice control](https://www.music-assistant.io/integration/voice/), [HA local engines](https://www.home-assistant.io/voice_control/voice_remote_local_assistant/), [legacy custom sentence lists](https://github.com/OHF-Voice/speech-to-phrase#custom-sentences).

Spotify need not require a new Pulsar player implementation: MA already documents a [Spotify source](https://www.music-assistant.io/music-providers/spotify/) and a separate [Spotify Connect plugin](https://www.music-assistant.io/plugins/spotify-connect/) that can expose MA players to the Spotify app. These are distinct features with their own account and setup constraints; the deferred Spotify ticket should define which one the owner wants. They were not configured or tested here.

## Validation and unknowns

- Inspect HA version, installation type, MA version and host capacity before choosing the exact app setup. Install/select an English Speech-to-Phrase model and local Piper voice; expose a suitable light/switch to Assist, associate the satellite with an area and pipeline, and confirm the response URL resolves and is reachable from the speaker. No existing HA configuration has been checked.
- Under the project's live-station safety protocol and with the owner's consent for sound, test wake followed by English command, same-utterance pre-roll versus a pause, entity action, English response, and recovery on HA disconnect/reconnect. Confirm the Russian wake model still works with English pipeline selection; this compatibility is inferred from architecture, not yet observed on this unit.
- Validate mute physically prevents capture and that wake, English commands, and local controls behave as specified; verify music playback, volume, play/pause, ducking, and response over music. Hardware and sound tests are outstanding. Separately assess whether offline operation means physical controls only: English HA commands inherently need HA, whereas documented local spoken commands are Russian.
- Confirm actual HA/MA versions' compatibility, discovery/network rules, TTS codecs/URL reachability, buffering/latency and music/voice interaction on the Mini 2. MA documents Sendspin as a technical preview with evolving pairing/legacy-client behavior, so verify rather than assume interoperability across versions. [MA Sendspin](https://www.music-assistant.io/player-support/sendspin/).
- Independently finish the network-services investigation: local speech does not imply the entire device has stopped contacting Yandex. Pulsar's README explicitly says firmware DNS, NTP and account binding remain. [Pulsar limitations](https://github.com/RisinT96/pulsar/blob/fd434437d2aa94a1c522df7ea8ae8ed0818d7de8/README.md#ограничения).
